# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

Duophonic doesn’t collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No data collection.** Duophonic has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking.
- **No network access.** The app makes no network connections. The only links in it, to this project and to System Settings, open in your browser or in System Settings.
- **No account.** There is nothing to sign up for.
- **What stays on your Mac.** Duophonic remembers the two outputs you picked, their names, and the outputs to restore when sharing stops, in its own preferences on your Mac. Removing the app and its preferences deletes them (see [Uninstall](README.md#uninstall)).
- **Audio.** Duophonic routes your Mac’s sound to the outputs you choose. It never records, analyzes or stores what you listen to.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.

Questions? Open an issue at [github.com/juri1212/Duophonic/issues](https://github.com/juri1212/Duophonic/issues).
