Duophonic is a menu bar app. It has no Dock icon and no main window: after launch, click its icon (a split cable) in the menu bar to open it.

How to test:
1. Connect a second audio output to the Mac, for example wired or Bluetooth headphones, or a display with speakers. The built-in speakers count as one output.
2. Open Duophonic from the menu bar. Click Output 1 and Output 2 to choose the two devices.
3. Turn on the Audio Sharing switch at the top and play any audio. It plays on both outputs. Each output has its own volume slider.
4. Turn the switch off, or quit with the Quit button. The Mac's previous output is restored.

While sharing, Duophonic creates a temporary multi-output device with the public Core Audio API (AudioHardwareCreateAggregateDevice) and removes it again when sharing stops.

The app needs no account and makes no network connections. It collects no data. "Open at Login" uses SMAppService and is off until the user turns it on.
